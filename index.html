A bit more about me

I came up through museum curatorial departments, then spent the better part of a decade running community programs out of storefronts, church basements and the occasional bus stop. Somewhere along the way I started recording the conversations, and that turned into a second career in audio.

I'm curious about almost everything, which makes me a decent interviewer and a slightly exhausting dinner guest. I like small teams, plain language, honest budgets and clear next steps. When I don't know something, I'll say so, and then I'll find the person who does.

I live in Pittsburgh, work with teams everywhere, and am always happy to grab a coffee or a call.

Your first podcast, step by step

If you've never made a show before, here's roughly how it goes. I'll keep you posted at every stage and flag decisions that are yours to make.

01

Discover

We start with goals and audience. Sometimes the honest answer is that a podcast isn't the right move, and I'll tell you if so.

02

Design

Format, name, hosts, budget and team, in a plain-language plan with real numbers.

03

Produce

I bring in and manage the right collaborators and keep the schedule moving. You approve at clear checkpoints.

04

Launch & learn

Trailer, distribution, a measurement plan that goes beyond downloads, and a debrief on what to change for next season.

Good questions

Do you work with small budgets?

Yes. I'll tell you what's realistic, where to spend and where to save, and I'd rather scope something smaller and good than bigger and strained.

Can you work with our agency or in-house team?

Absolutely. I'm happy to slot in alongside your people, whether that means leading, supporting, or just being a thought partner.
